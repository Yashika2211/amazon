Not a medical device

KinLoop is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, and it does not replace emergency services, doctors or pharmacists. It never calls emergency services. In an emergency, call 112 (India) or 911 (US) yourself.

Alerts can fail

Alerts depend on Alexa, the internet, AWS, email and SMS providers. Any of them can be delayed or fail. Don't rely on KinLoop as the only way someone will know you need help.

Your responsibilities

  • Only set up KinLoop for a person who has agreed to it.
  • Keep sign-in names and PINs private.
  • Medicine schedules you enter are your responsibility; check them with a doctor or pharmacist.

Demo data

The demo household (“Kamala” and “Arjun”) is fictional. Don't enter real health information into the public demo.
